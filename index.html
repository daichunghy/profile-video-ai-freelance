05

Fair questions

The things clients actually ask before the first order.

Is AI content safe for YouTube monetization?

No producer can guarantee monetization — anyone who promises it isn't being straight with you. What I can say: YouTube's reused-content policy targets copy-paste, mass-produced automation, not original AI-assisted production, which is how I work: original researched scripts, human-directed structure and pacing, no mass-replicated templates. My own channels run this exact workflow.

Why are you cheaper than other producers?

Two honest reasons: I'm based in Vietnam, and I'm buying my first reviews with price. The trial is cheap because a finished video on your topic convinces better than any pitch. Rates rise as the order book fills — early clients lock today's pricing.

How is my video priced?

By video type and complexity, on the fixed tiers above — a simple build sits at the lower tier for its format; a complex build (many scenes, heavy motion graphics, elaborate setups) at the higher one. Extras beyond the package, such as an additional subtitle language, an extra thumbnail, rush delivery or another format, are quoted as itemised add-on lines in your quote before production starts. No hidden charges, and the price does not change by country.

Which niches do you take?

Storytelling, history, documentary, true crime, paranormal, geography, finance explainers and language education are proven fits. If your niche needs on-camera presence or live footage, I'll tell you upfront instead of taking the job.

What do you need from me to start?

A niche, a topic (or I can propose three), one reference channel, and the target length. That's it — script approval is your only checkpoint before delivery.

How do we work together?

Email or your platform of choice (Upwork/Fiverr contracts welcome — escrow protects us both). I'm on GMT+7 and reply within 12 hours; delivery windows are quoted in your timezone.
